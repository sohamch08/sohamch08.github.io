# Hi, I am Soham Chatterjee.

I am a third-year Integrated Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai](https://www.tcs.tifr.res.in/web/). I am currently being advised by Prof. [Mrinal Kumar](https://mrinalkr.bitbucket.io/). I completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science at [Chennai Mathematical Institute](https://www.cmi.ac.in/) (2021–2024).

My primary research interests lie in Theoretical Computer Science. Broadly, I am interested in:

- Algorithms and Complexity Theory (with an algebraic flavor)
- Error-Correcting Codes
- Pseudorandomness
- Algebra and Computation
- Algebraic Complexity Theory
- Computational Complexity Theory

Beyond research work, I enjoy typesetting my notes in LaTeX. It has become a favorite hobby of mine over the years. I have been using it for more than five years and have created a [custom theme](https://github.com/sohamch08/lumina) with which I typeset my lecture notes and reports.
