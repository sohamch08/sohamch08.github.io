## Research Internshipts

### Summer 2024

Worked on derandomization of isolation lemma over polytopes under prof. [Rohit Gurjar](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~rgurjar/), IIT Bombay

In this project I tried to extend the idea of bounding the number of vectors in the integer lattice which has $L_1$ norm less than twice the shortest vector for 0-1 matrices with $k$-column sum to derandomize isolation lemma over more general polytopes. Additionally I read about isolating a path connecting in a black-box layered graph.

### Dec'23-Jul'24

Quantum Property Testing of Junta Functions and Partially Symmetric Functions with prof. [Arijit Ghosh](https://sites.google.com/site/homepagearijitghosh/), ISI Kolkata

In this project I learned about Quantum Boolean Functions and some basics of Quantum algorithms for property testing from the Survey by [Montanaro-Osborne, 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.2435). I learned about Classical and Quantum Junta Function testing. We worked on finding a more efficient Quantum Algorithm for testing Paritally symmetric boolean functions by improving the classical algorithm in Blais-Weinstein-Yoshida's Paper.

### Summer 2023

Reading project on Factorization of Arithmetic Circuits under prof. [Nitin Saxena](https://www.cse.iitk.ac.in/users/nitin/), IIT Kanpur

In the project I learned factorization techniques and closure of of VP and VBP under factorization. I also read closure of VF with bounded individual degree from Oliviera, 2016 paper and we worked on removing the bounded individual degree condition

### Dec 2022

Computational Number Theroy and Algebra for Algebraic Comlexity Theory under prof. [Nitin Saxena](https://www.cse.iitk.ac.in/users/nitin/), IIT Kanpur

I did a basic study of Computational Number Theory and Algebra from Nitin Saxena's course and about Arithmetic Circuits from [Amir Shpilka's Survey](https://www.nowpublishers.com/article/Details/TCS-039) and [Ramprasad Saptharishi's Survey](https://github.com/dasarpmar/lowerbounds-survey) on Arithmetic Circuits.
